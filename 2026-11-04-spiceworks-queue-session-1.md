spicework-queue session

Goal: Work a practice queue in my own Spiceworks Cloud Help Desk-- triage clarifying questions, diagnosis, and document resolutions, ticket by ticket.

What I did: Entered each ticket the way a user would(unassigned, default priority, no category) then assigned it to myself, set priority and category, posted clarifying questions before diagnosing, and closed with a root-causse and prevention note 
     Ticket 1- Account lockout (accounting user, back from vacation, QuickBooks deadline): Set priority to high (fully blocked + hard deadline) Category: other -- my instance has no Account Access category yet, adding proper categories is on my list. The user's answers showed she'd changed her password the Friday before vacation and never updated it on her phone, which kept failing sign-ins with the old password until the account locked. Fix order mattered: verify identity--> update the phones password first --> unlock the account --> confirm login and QuickBooks access --> close with the prevention note ("update your phone the same day you changed your password").

     What I got wrong: Not giving her a follow up message at first which I ended up doing

     What I learned: A ticketing system records work that happens elsewhere (admin consoles, user devices)-- the diagnosis and the write-up are the job. And the fix order is part of the fix: unlock before fixing the phone and the account just locks again.
